# 2026-08-09

## Summary

Created ROM GUARD and completed its first Cloudflare Pages deployment.
The initial idea was a localized Xiaomi ROM reference site. Two demand checks
(total cost: $0.25) showed that it would not support a viable revenue business.
Preventing a single bricked device was considered worthwhile regardless of demand;
the project therefore remains nonprofit and free of advertising.

## Measurements

Results were recorded in `out/gaps-2026-08-09-*.json` by the search-analysis tooling.

| ROM flashing / bootloader unlock | 1,470 across 110 keywords | Forums and publishing platforms | Potential visibility, little revenue |

Retailers already occupied the accessory results that affiliate traffic would target.
The lesson was recorded as `feedback_serp_slot_check`.

## Completed

- Required source URLs and verification dates for non-null values in validation;
  rejected mirror URLs and hashes copied from upstream listings.
- Generated risk and device pages directly from JSON to prevent content drift.
- Checked all five deployed pages with headless Chromium; no console errors.
- Deployed Cloudflare Pages project `romguard`; no announcement before a custom domain.
- Registered checks for verification dates older than 180 days and site availability.

suggestions. Its $0.002/SERP estimate was corrected after actual costs proved ten
