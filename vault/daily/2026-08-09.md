# 2026-08-09

## Summary

Created ROM GUARD and completed its first Cloudflare Pages deployment.

The initial idea was a localized Xiaomi ROM reference site. Two DataForSEO demand checks (total $0.25)
showed it would **not support a viable revenue business**. Preventing even one bricked device
was considered worthwhile regardless of demand. The project remains nonprofit and advertising-free.

## Measurements (search-analysis tooling; out/gaps-2026-08-09-*.json)

| Topic | Monthly searches | Leading results | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| ROM flashing / bootloader unlock | 1,470 (110 keywords) | reddit / hatena / note | Potential visibility, little revenue |
| Cases / accessories | 22,530 (115 keywords) | Retailers and marketplaces | Demand, but little room for a reference site |

Retailers already occupy the accessory search results that affiliate traffic would target,
leaving little room for an information site. Recorded as `feedback_serp_slot_check`.
