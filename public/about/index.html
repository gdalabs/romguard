About

A non-commercial dataset covering the one part of Xiaomi ROM flashing that nobody has organised: whether the thing you are about to do can be undone. Run by GDA Labs. No ads, no affiliate links.

Scope

In scopeReversibility (ARB, EDL, relocking). What unlocking costs you, per region. Verified per-device facts. Hash records for distributed ROM files.
Out of scopeFlashing instructions. ROM file distribution. Recommending a particular custom ROM.

Why there are no instructions here

Instructions become obsolete quickly and mistakes can cause the most serious damage. Obsolete instructions can cause an ARB violation that leaves a device irreparable. Read primary sources such as xiaomi.eu, XDA, and the LineageOS wiki for instructions. This site helps assess whether an operation can be undone before following those sources.

Why no ROM files are mirrored

Three reasons; any one of them is sufficient.

  1. Redistribution rights cannot be assumed. Xiaomi owns the official ROM copyright; its EULA restricts redistribution. For modified ROMs such as xiaomi.eu, redistribution rights cannot be assumed upstream or downstream.
  2. Source obligations may apply. Distributing binaries containing the Linux kernel can require providing corresponding source.
  3. A mirror becomes a distributor. Hosting the file makes the mirror responsible for distributing its contents, This differs from simply relaying a user post. A malicious ROM can cause operational damage even before legal liability is considered: Safe Browsing warnings and hosting suspension can make the site unavailable.

Every large ROM database states "we don't host files, we only link to official sources." That is not modesty.

Why blank cells are left blank

arb and felica require a physical device or fastboot ROM metadata to verify. Currently 60% of the device dataset is unverified. A complete-looking table is less important than refusing guesses that could damage a device.

Setting a value requires source (URL) and verified_at (date). This is not a convention described in prose — scripts/validate.mjs rejects the build otherwise, and warns on anything verified more than 180 days ago.

Contributing

The data is the JSON in the GitHub repository; the site is a view of it. Pull requests filling in a single field are welcome. Most fields can only be filled by someone holding the device, so this dataset will not be finished by one person.

Legal

Using a device without required local conformity certification may violate applicable radio laws. Changing a ROM may also affect manufacturer warranty coverage. This site does not recommend these actions; it documents their technical consequences.
