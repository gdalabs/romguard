Before you flash, check whether you can undo it

The worst outcome of flashing a Xiaomi is not a device that fails to boot. It is a device nobody can ever repair. There are only two ways to get there, and both are avoidable — but only before you flash. This is not a how-to. It is the check you run first.

What this answers, and what it does not

AnswersWhether the operation is reversible. The ARB and EDL conditions. What unlocking costs you in your region. Whether a downloaded file matches what was recorded.
Does not answerHow to flash. Instructions rot fastest and do the most damage when wrong, so they are left to the upstream sources.
Does not hostROM files. No redistribution right, and a poisoned mirror would make us the publisher. Why

Regions

Xiaomi ships different firmware per market, and what you lose by leaving your region's build differs by country — carrier VoLTE profiles and band tables everywhere, FeliCa on Japanese units, extra account-binding on mainland-China units. Devices bought abroad and used on a domestic network are the ones most likely to lose something quietly.

Local law also differs. Importing or operating a device without your country's radio-equipment certification may be an offence where you live. Check before you buy, not after you flash.

Coverage

Blank cells are shown as blank. Some fields cannot be confirmed without the physical device, and this project does not fill those in by guessing.

Start here

  • Anti-Rollback (ARB) violation — burned into an eFuse. No recovery exists.
  • Dropping to EDL (9008) — needs an authorized Mi Account you almost certainly do not have.
  • Relocking on a custom ROM — will not boot; recoverable while fastboot lives.
